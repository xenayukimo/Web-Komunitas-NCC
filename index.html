About Us

Knights of the Silent Road

NIGHT CAVALRY CLUB, or 夜鉄騎団, was born from a deep love for Japan's golden era of automotive culture in the 90s — a time when every body line, livery, and engine note carried its own character. We are riders who chose the quiet path: gathering and running together through the darkness of night, carrying the spirit of an iron cavalry loyal to a single direction.

Our name draws inspiration from 鉄馬騎兵団, the iron horse cavalry corps — a symbol of discipline and honor that forms the foundation of every member who joins. To us, a car is never just a vehicle; it's a statement of dedication to detail, style, and respect for classic JDM history.

Honor

Every member upholds the identity and concept built together, never personal interest.

Precision

Detail, fitment, and livery are refined down to the smallest part, true to the 90's JDM spirit.

Loyalty

Quality always comes first, over both member count and car count.

Rules

Membership Terms & International Recruitment

Night Cavalry Club — Open International Recruitment

Night Cavalry Club is officially opening recruitment for players outside Indonesia. We're looking for players who really understand 90s JDM style and quality builds.

Recruitment Requirements

Applicants must submit one of these requirements:

5 RAW photos & 5 edited photos
or
1 edit video

These requirements are used to see your style, quality, and overall effort.

Member Rules

  • 90s JDM style is a must
  • Neon/underglow is not allowed
  • Headlights must use the original in-game headlights, no custom biled headlights
  • Fitment and overall build should match the 90s style
  • 90s-style liveries are highly recommended
  • Cars with non-90s liveries may not be included in club content
  • Overly modern/futuristic styles are not allowed
  • AI-generated edits/content are not allowed
  • Keep the clean look and street racing atmosphere

Additional Information

  • If a member no longer fits the club's goals, concept, or rules, the founder has the right to remove the player without prior notice.
  • Founder decisions are final to maintain the club's quality and identity.
  • Outside of official club activities, the rules above can be ignored for personal use or freeplay.

Notes

Night Cavalry Club cares more about quality than quantity. Joining the club means you're ready to follow the concept and identity built together by the community.
